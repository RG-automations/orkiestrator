# Video script format (reels, shorts, TikTok)
- 20-30 seconds, 4 to 6 scenes. One scene = one caption + 1-2 short spoken sentences.
- Scene 1 names the viewer's problem in the first second. No "Cześć, dzisiaj powiem...".
- Last scene: the single call to action from `offer/offer.md`.
- Fields of each scene: `t` (seconds), `say` (Polish, short sentences), `on_screen` (max 6 words), `visual` (what the camera shows).
- No prices in videos. Only facts from the offer files.
